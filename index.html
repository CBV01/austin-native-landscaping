Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I choose native plants for my Texas landscape?

Native plants are naturally adapted to our Texas climate, requiring less water, fertilizer, and maintenance. They support local ecosystems, attract pollinators, and create landscapes that thrive in our unique weather conditions.

How often should I schedule landscape maintenance?

Most Texas properties benefit from monthly maintenance during growing season and quarterly check-ups year-round. We'll create a customized schedule based on your specific landscape needs and plant types.

Do you offer sustainable watering solutions?

Yes, we specialize in water-efficient irrigation systems and drought-resistant plant selections. Our designs typically reduce water usage by 30-50% compared to traditional landscapes while maintaining beauty and health.

What's included in your free consultation?

Our consultation includes a property assessment, discussion of your goals, preliminary design ideas, and a transparent estimate. There's no obligation - it's an opportunity to explore how we can transform your outdoor space.
